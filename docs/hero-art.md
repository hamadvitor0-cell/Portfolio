# Hero comercial — Viham Dev

Arte original criada com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. Não foi usada API/CLI de fallback.

Asset de produção: `public/images/hero/viham-web-studio.webp`.

A ilustração representa o serviço oferecido. Os projetos reais continuam identificados na seção seguinte; a arte não é apresentada como cliente, aplicação entregue ou resultado comercial.

O PNG gerado foi somente redimensionado e codificado em WebP para uso no site, preservando o canal alpha. A logo aprovada continua aplicada separadamente no cabeçalho.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: production hero artwork for Viham Dev, an independent Brazilian website development studio. This is an illustrative service visual, not a customer project or proof of results.
Primary request: create a striking, elegant premium 3D editorial cutout showing a professional website working beautifully on desktop and smartphone. The art will occupy a large right column beside live HTML sales copy on a solid dark navy website.
Subject: one large ivory desktop browser window in a restrained three-quarter perspective, with a slim contemporary smartphone standing in front of its lower-right portion. The browser shows a sophisticated minimal service-business website: bold dark navy typography represented ONLY by clean short graphic bars, generous white space, one vivid lime call-to-action button, a large editorial architectural photograph of sculptural white and dark concrete, and a restrained bottom row of content blocks. The phone clearly echoes the same responsive layout. Add one bold sculptural lime 3D mouse cursor near the contact button, large enough to be an intentional part of the composition.
Style: meticulous high-end product advertising render, crisp edges, believable materials, soft studio highlights, sophisticated understated depth. Not cartoon, not clay, not a stock office photo.
Composition: nearly square canvas, the browser is the dominant object and the phone overlaps naturally. Use the available frame generously; all devices and cursor fully contained with a small safe margin. Balanced composition that remains recognizable when reduced to 340 pixels wide. No text outside the devices. No decorative cloud, no extra objects.
Palette: midnight navy #101C36, slightly deeper navy #0B1529, clean ivory #F7F8F5, lime #D5F662 as a vivid selective accent.
Background: genuinely transparent background. Isolated objects with subtle soft contact shadows suitable for compositing on #101C36. No rectangular background or surrounding panel.
Constraints: no readable words, letters, numbers, company names, logos, watermarks, charts, graphs, revenue figures, testimonial portraits, fictitious client brands, code, grids, robots, or brains. No purple or orange. No neon glow, light halo, lens flare, aurora, decorative gradients, or floating badges. Preserve high clarity of the product silhouette.
```
